# Timeline of the 'Verse

`(?)` = date or name not fully verified.

## Age of Expansion (pre-UEE)

| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| 21st c. | **Roberts Space Industries (RSI)** founded on Earth. Builds the first practical space engines. |
| ~2075 | RSI's quantum drive makes in-system travel cheap. Humanity spreads through Sol. |
| 22nd c. | **Mars terraformed** by RSI. It's a showcase for the terraforming industry that later remakes hundreds of worlds. |
| **2271** | **Nick Croshaw discovers the first jump point** (Sol → Croshaw). Interstellar expansion begins. Croshaw is the first human system outside Sol. |
| 2380 | **United Nations of Earth (UNE)** formed as the first unified human government across systems. |
| **2438** | **First contact: the Banu.** Explorer Vernon Tar meets a fleeing Banu ship in Davien. Two weeks later Banu Protectorate envoys arrive and a trade partnership begins. It's humanity's first alien friendship. |
| 2523 | UNE reorganised into the **United Planets of Earth (UPE)**. |
| **2530** | **First contact: the Xi'an.** Relations are tense from the start, which leads to a centuries-long **cold war**. |
| 2541 | **First Tevarin War.** The nomadic, honour-bound Tevarin, led by Corath'Thal, attack humanity. |
| 2544 | **Castra discovered** and fortified as a military bastion (it later borders Xi'an space). |
| **2546** | The Tevarin are defeated. War hero **Ivar Messer** uses the crisis to take power: the UPE becomes the **United Empire of Earth (UEE)**, with Messer as first **Imperator**. |

## The Messer Era (2546 – 2792): the dark age

A dynasty of authoritarian Imperators. Propaganda, a surveillance state, militarism, and dissidents
who "disappeared". Senators from Terra who spoke for secession tended to vanish.

| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| 2603–2610 | **Second Tevarin War.** The Tevarin return to retake their old homeworld **Elysium IV (Kaleeth)**. At the end, the last Tevarin fleet flies into the planet's defensive shields and burns up rather than surrender. The survivors are absorbed into the UEE as a scattered, second-class diaspora. |
| 2618 | Activists and refugees fleeing the Messers settle a vacated mining asteroid in **Nyx's Glaciem Ring**. It becomes **Levski**, home of the **People's Alliance**. |
| 26th–28th c. | The **Xi'an cold war** is at its peak. Castra and other border systems become fortresses. **Stanton**, **Pyro** and others are explored and claimed. Pyro's corporate colony fails and the system is abandoned to outlaws. |
| **2681** | **First contact: the Vanduul.** They attack the human colony in **Orion** with no diplomacy and no warning. |
| 2681–2712 | The first major Vanduul raid wave: **Orion, Caliban and Virgil fall** or are abandoned. **Tiber** becomes a meat-grinder front for the next 250 years, costing millions of lives. |
| ~2788–2789 | The **Garron II massacre** scandal: the Messer government knowingly terraformed a world that held a primitive native species and wiped it out. Exposure triggers mass unrest. |
| 2789 | The UEE scales back its military in Castra as a goodwill gesture to the Xi'an. Civilians are allowed in. |
| **2792** | **Fall of the Messer regime.** Mass protests and a popular uprising topple the last Messer Imperator. Democratic reforms follow, and peace accords with the Xi'an end the cold war in practice. |

## The Modern UEE (2792 – 2956)

| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| 28th–29th c. | Rebuilding and reform. A cash-strapped UEE starts **selling whole planets to megacorporations**. **Stanton** is carved up between Hurston Dynamics, Crusader Industries, ArcCorp and microTech, which is why Stanton is "corporate space". |
| 29th c. | The **Synthworld** project begins: an artificial planet being built by the UEE. It's an enormous, over-budget and politically loaded megaproject. |
| ongoing | Vanduul raids along the western frontier (Tiber, Vega, Virgil-adjacent space). **Hadesian** ruins (a dead civilisation that destroyed itself) are studied in the Hades system. |
| 2939–2950 | Imperator **Kelos Cole**'s term(s). (?) |
| **2945** | **Vega II attacked by the Vanduul.** This is the opening of **Squadron 42** (the single-player campaign): a rookie pilot joins the UEE Navy's 42nd Squadron aboard the Idris **UEES Stanton**. Cast includes Mark Hamill (Lt. Cmdr. Steve "Old Man" Colton), Gary Oldman (Admiral Ernst Bishop) and Gillian Anderson (Captain Rachel MacLaren). |
| 2950 | **Imperatorial election.** **Laylani Addison**, an independent and former Dean of the University of Mentor, wins over candidates including Terra's **Senator Mira Ngo** (Transitionalist, wanted to move the capital to Terra). Proclaimed 1 Nov 2950. |
| **2951** | **Addison sworn in** (1 Jan 2951). Agenda: education, scientific research, and **loosening restrictions on AI research**. That last one is controversial and a good DM seed. |
| 2953–2956 | The **live PU era**: Nine Tails lockdowns, XenoThreat, the Siege of Orison, Pyro opens, the Slicers / Frontier Fighters saga, the Regen Crisis, Nyx opens, the Molina Mold outbreak. Full detail in [04-live-story-arcs.md](04-live-story-arcs.md). |
| 2956 | **Now.** An ongoing Vanduul war. A rising Vanduul presence near the Virgil–Nyx jump. The People's Alliance at a crossroads. The ASD / regen scandal still not settled. |

## Looking ahead (developer-stated)

- **Squadron 42** is targeted for end of 2026, with a press preview in Manchester, 9–11 Oct 2026.
  Its story (2945–2946) is backstory for the PU.
- **Star Citizen 1.0** plans an NPC-driven **main story** built around **growing tension between Terra and Earth**,
  set across the "First Five" systems: Stanton, Pyro, Nyx, Castra and Terra.
