Each lists the **playable activity** it maps to.

## Evergreen story seeds

- **The debt contract**: a Hurston worker begs the crew to smuggle their family out of Lorville. Hurston Security hunts them across Stanton.
- **Ghost signal**: a distress beacon from a Messer-era ship in the Keeger Belt. Inside: data on the Garron II cover-up.
- **The Tevarin heirloom**: a Tevarin elder wants a war relic recovered from a gang vault at Ruin Station. Honour demands it isn't bought.
- **Xenophobe cell**: XenoThreat survivors plan an attack on Xi'an traders during an Alien Week-style festival.
- **Banu bargain**: Wikelo offers an impossible trade if the crew brings back something that "doesn't exist" (an Apex Valakkar pearl).
- **Clean slate**: a CrimeStat-wiped identity is for sale in Levski, but it belonged to someone who died in the regen failures.

## Campaign "end goal" patterns (to wire into the app)

A campaign should have **one end goal**, 3–5 acts, and clear in-game objectives per session:
1. **Uncover** (mystery): e.g. find Jorrit. Clues come from Onyx sites, then Pyro labs, then Nyx. The finale is a confrontation.
2. **Build** (economy): e.g. save Levski. Haul aid, fund the repairs, arm the station before the Vanduul raid.
3. **Rise** (faction): e.g. become the Headhunters' (or CFP's) top crew and take a Pyro station.
4. **Hunt** (bounty): e.g. bring down the Frontier Fighter successor cell, one lieutenant per session.
5. **Escape/Protect** (escort): e.g. get the Hurston debt family to freedom in Nyx.

Progress should track real things players can show: aUEC earned, cargo SCU delivered, reputation ranks, bounties
completed, locations visited, and items or loot recovered.
