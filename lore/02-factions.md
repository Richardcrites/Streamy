# Factions of the 'Verse

## Human government: the United Empire of Earth (UEE)

- **Imperator**: elected head of state. Currently **Laylani Addison** (since 2951): an academic who
  pushes science, education and loosening the AI-research restrictions.
- **Imperial Senate**: one senator per represented system. The "Transitionalists" want the capital moved
  to Terra. Earth-versus-Terra politics is the backbone of the planned 1.0 main story.
- **UEE Navy**: fleet and fighter wings. The **42nd Squadron** (Squadron 42) is its most famous unit.
- **UEE Marines**: boarding and ground forces.
- **Advocacy**: federal law enforcement and investigators (the UEE's FBI).
- **Civilian Defense Force (CDF)**: volunteer militia that recruits ordinary citizens. It's the player's way
  into Siege of Orison and the Save Stanton investigations.
- **UEE planetary tiers**: full UEE citizenship is earned (by service, for example). Civilians lack the
  vote. Citizen vs civilian is a good source of character motive.

### Non-UEE human factions

- **People's Alliance** (Nyx, HQ **Levski**): egalitarian, self-sufficient, anti-authoritarian.
  Founded by refugees from the Messer regime. They're in a **cold war with the UEE**, but rising Vanduul
  activity may push them to ask the Empire for help. Their government is struggling: Levski's infrastructure
  is decaying (see Molina Mold).
- **Vox Populi**: a Nyx political faction (related to the Alliance's internal politics). (?)

## Alien species

### Xi'an (Xi'an Empire)
- Very long-lived and patient. They plan in centuries. Formal and protocol-driven.
- Cold war with humanity (2530s–2792) that ended in peace accords after the Messers fell. Now cautious
  trading partners. Still guarded.
- Ships: **Aopoa** (Khartu-al, Nox, San'tok.yāi) and **Gatac** (Syulen; **Railen** freighter released at Alien Week 2026).
  MISC builds human ships with Xi'an tech.
- DM use: diplomatic intrigue, old grudges from Castra, xenophobe targets.

### Banu (Banu Protectorate)
- Humanity's first alien friends (2438). A loose society of independent city-worlds ("Souli") and guilds.
- Merchants and traders above all. They'll trade with anyone, including outlaws. Copyright and ownership are fuzzy
  concepts to them.
- **Wikelo**: a Banu trader in the PU who runs **Wikelo's Emporium**. Exotic barter quests (Storm Breaker).
- Ships: Banu Defender, Banu Merchantman.

### Tevarin
- A once-proud warrior race defeated in two wars (2541–46, 2603–10). Their homeworld Kaleeth is now
  **Elysium IV**. They live as a diaspora inside the UEE, keeping their culture and their honour code.
- Ships rebuilt by Esperia: Prowler, Talon.
- DM use: honour, lost homeland, prejudice, old war relics.

### Vanduul
- No known diplomacy. Clan-based raiders who strip worlds bare. Males live on ships in "hordes"/clans.
- 16 known systems held, including **Caliban, Orion, Tiber, Virgil**, Vagabond, Vanguard, Vector, Vendetta, Veritas,
  Vermilion, Vesper, Viking, Virgo, Volt, Voodoo, Vulture.
- First contact 2681 (Orion). Attacked **Vega** in 2945 (Squadron 42). **2956: an ongoing Vanduul war**, with
  Vanduul ships massing near the **Virgil–Nyx jump point**. **Nyx brought the first real Vanduul encounters into the PU.**
- Ships: Scythe, Glaive, Blade, Kingship.
- One Vanduul was held captive by ASD (Project Hyperion). What happened to it is unclear.

### Others and extinct
- **Hadesians**: a dead civilisation. Their system (**Hades**) has ruins and a world shattered by their own weapons.
- **Kr'Thak**: an alien race mentioned in older lore; details are thin. (?)
- Wildlife: Kopion, Marok, Valakkar (giant sandworms, with an **Apex Valakkar** in Storm Breaker), Yormandi
  (a giant creature linked to the Hyperion dossier).
