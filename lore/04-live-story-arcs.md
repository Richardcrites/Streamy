# Live Story Arcs (the "current" lore)

These story events have played out in the PU since about 2953 in-game (2023 in real life). They're
what NPCs in the 'Verse would actually be talking about.

## 2953 — Stanton under siege
- **Nine Tails Lockdown**: the Nine Tails blockade Stanton stations and players break the siege.
- **XenoThreat**: human-supremacist militants come through the **Pyro jump point** and attack Stanton.
  Players run supply lines and fight back.
- **Jumptown**: a drug-lab turf war (WiDoW and other drugs) in Stanton where outlaws and lawful players fight for control.
- **Siege of Orison**: the Nine Tails seize Orison's platforms. The CDF calls in civilians.
  *(Reworked in Alpha 4.10, Aug 2026, as a permanent, instanced 4-player FPS mission: three platforms in the
  Inspiration Park cluster, kill Commander **Mendo Ren**, disable the **IFF inverters** that jam Crusader Security.)*
- **Blockade Runner**: run cargo through hostile blockades.

## 2954 — Save Stanton / the Slicers (Alpha 3.24–4.0)
- A new gang, the **Slicers**, attacks Stanton: ships hit, cargo stolen. Players defend the system.
- **The twist (revealed early 2955, Alpha 4.0.2):** the CDF found the Slicers were **undercover Frontier Fighters**.
  The FF staged the attacks to scare people and win support for their "invade Pyro and wipe out the gangs" agenda.
  The worst damage was done by the FF themselves.
- The Slicers were designated terrorists. The Headhunters and Citizens for Prosperity started an offensive (**Supply or Die**).

## 2955 — Pyro Must Burn (Alpha 4.0 → 4.3.2)
- **Pyro opens** (Alpha 4.0, Dec 2954/2024). There's a **Fight for Pyro** between the gangs and the CFP.
- **Storm Breaker** (Alpha 4.2): investigate ASD data centres on **Pyro IV**, then break through **Pyro I**'s radioactive storms into
  locked ASD labs. Clues to Dr. Jorrit's methods, mutated lifeforms, the **Apex Valakkar**, and Wikelo's barter trade.
- **Onyx Facilities** (Alpha 4.3, Aug 2955/2025): about 100 abandoned ASD sites across Stanton's moons. **Hockrow Agency**
  (lead investigator **Arken Mallor**) hires citizens to investigate.
- **Frontier Fighters Finale** (Alpha 4.3.2, Oct 2955/2025): players choose a side, the **Headhunters** or the **CFP**, to hunt
  **Amelia Boyd**.
  - **Outcome: the Headhunters won.** They tracked Boyd into Stanton, captured her before the CFP could hand her to UEE authorities,
    and **publicly executed her**. Justice was denied, but the Frontier Fighters lost their head.

## 2955–2956 — the Regen Crisis / ASD scandal
- **Regen (respawn) tech is failing** for some people. They don't come back. There's public panic.
- **Project Hyperion** (from the Jorrit Dossier missions): ASD held a **captured Vanduul** and a **human subject who was killed and
  regenerated again and again**, all to improve regen. Links to **Yormandi** creature research.
- **Dr. Logan Jorrit** was put on administrative leave and is now **missing**. Hints say he left more behind than his research.
- **Science Runner Muk** storyline (around Alpha 4.4): more exposure of shady corporate activity tied to the regen crisis.
- CitizenCon 2955/2025 said the Regen Crisis arc was "wrapping up". There's no clear public ending yet, so treat it as **open**.

## Late 2955 — Welcome to Nyx (Alpha 4.4, Nov 2955/2025)
- **Nyx and Levski open.** Interstellar hauling. Investigations of abandoned stations.
- The People's Alliance is at a crossroads, in a cold war with the UEE, and **Vanduul are massing near the Virgil jump**.
- New factions: **Intersec Defense Solutions**, **Shattered Blade**, and the first Vanduul fights in the PU.

## Late 2955 — Dawn of Engineering (Alpha 4.5, Dec 2955/2025)
- Ship engineering arrives (power, repairs, crew roles). Related events: **Fight for Stanton**, **Resource Drive Initiative**, and the ASD storyline continues.

## 2956 (real 2026) so far
- **Alliance Aid** (Alpha 4.6): the **Molina Mold** epidemic hits Levski. It's an airborne sickness that resists treatment, caused by
  **faulty Gyson air filtration**. Players haul medical and filter supplies, collect materials, and defend convoys.
  This bridges into the **Levski Municipal Works** (missions inside Levski to repair broken systems).
- **Alpha 4.7** (spring): the second Nyx mission pack. Factions move onto reputation. Couriering returns in Stanton and Pyro. Crafting begins.
- **Alpha 4.8 / 4.8.2** (June–July): **Alien Week 2956**, a celebration of Xi'an, Banu and Tevarin culture. The **Gatac Railen** Xi'an freighter arrives,
  with Vanduul Swarm rewards.
- **Alpha 4.9**: new defence missions.
- **Alpha 4.10 "Siege of Orison"** (Aug 26, 2026): instanced Siege of Orison. **4.10.1 "Orison Relief"** (Sept 19).
- **Alpha 4.10.2** (about Oct 7–8, 2026): a **new Stanton in-system event** (transport, collection, delivery and defence around Stanton's moon platforms,
  with a mobiGlas event tab and reward track). Narrative details were still emerging at time of writing.
- **Coming**: Castra (Alpha 4.11), Nyx planets via Genesis tech, the Squadron 42 preview (Oct 9–11, 2026), and the 1.0 Earth-versus-Terra main story.
