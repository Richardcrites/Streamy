# Locations

Status tags: **[PLAYABLE]** in the live game now · **[COMING]** announced for 1.0 · **[LORE]** exists only in lore.

## Stanton [PLAYABLE] — lawful, corporate space
The UEE sold Stanton's four planets to megacorps. It's a lawful system with Crusader Security, Hurston
security and the Advocacy on patrol.

| Body | Owner | Key locations |
|---|---|---|
| **Hurston** (+ moons Arial, Aberdeen, Magda, Ita) | Hurston Dynamics | **Lorville** (company city, smog, the Teasa Spaceport). |
| **Crusader** (+ moons Cellin, Daymar, Yela) | Crusader Industries | **Orison**: floating platforms in the clouds (target of the Siege of Orison). **GrimHEX**: a pirate station in the Yela asteroid belt. |
| **ArcCorp** (+ moons Lyria, Wala) | ArcCorp | **Area18**: a neon city that covers the planet. |
| **microTech** (+ moons Calliope, Clio, Euterpe) | microTech | **New Babbage**: a glass city on an ice world. |
| Moons across Stanton | — | **~100 Onyx Facilities**: abandoned ASD research sites (Regen Crisis / Jorrit Dossier missions). |

Other: Lagrange-point stations, **Kareah** (old security station), derelict sites, mining fields, and a jump point to Pyro, Nyx and others.

## Pyro [PLAYABLE since Alpha 4.0] — lawless frontier
A violent flare star with six planets and no law. A failed corporate colony left behind gangs and desperate settlers.
High risk, high reward.

- **Pyro I**: radioactive polar storms that hide ASD research facilities (Storm Breaker event).
- **Monox**, **Bloom**, **Pyro IV** (ASD data centres), **Pyro V**, **Terminus**.
- **Ruin Station**: the gang-run hub. Also Checkmate, Orbituary, Patch City, Gaslight and other stations, with
  control contested between the Headhunters, Rough & Ready, CFP and others.
- Contested zones and gang reputation decide who will deal with you.

## Nyx [PLAYABLE since Alpha 4.4] — unclaimed, outside UEE law
A cold, dark-nebula-edge system with asteroid fields. It's for those who want to live beyond the UEE's reach.

- **Levski**: a mining facility in the **Glaciem Ring** asteroid belt (on **Delamar**), and HQ of the **People's Alliance**.
  It's the central hub. In 2956 it's in crisis: the **Molina Mold** outbreak, failing air filtration, and the
  **Levski Municipal Works** repair zones.
- **Glaciem Ring (Belt Alpha)**: dense. Outlaw raiders base inside it.
- **Keeger Belt (Belt Beta)**: sparse. Smugglers use abandoned mining platforms as drop points.
- **Nyx I** (hero planet), **Nyx II** (toxic hellhole), **Nyx III** (gas giant): planets arrive during 2026 with "Genesis" planet tech.
- Jump points include **Virgil (Vanduul space!)**, Pyro, Stanton and Castra.
- Factions: People's Alliance, Intersec Defense Solutions, Shattered Blade, outlaws, **Vanduul**.

## Castra [COMING, Alpha 4.11 / 2026–27] — lawful, military
- Discovered in 2544 and terraformed for military use. The name comes from the Roman word for a fortified camp.
- It held a jump point into **Xi'an** space, so it was a cold-war fortress. A huge orbital platform supported capital ships.
- Demilitarised in part in 2789 and opened to civilians.
- **Castra II, "Cascom"**: the fortress city of **Sherman**, carved into **Mount Ulysses**.
- DM use: old military secrets, Xi'an tensions, war relics, bunkers.

## Terra [COMING] — lawful, the "second Earth"
- Planets: Aero, Pike, **Terra (Terra Prime)**, Gen. Wealthy, cosmopolitan and influential. Home of the **Terra Gazette** news outlet.
- The **Earth-versus-Terra rivalry** (capital move, secession history under the Messers) is the planned **1.0 main story**.

## Notable lore-only systems [LORE]
- **Sol**: Earth, the capital. Mars was terraformed. Heavily restricted.
- **Croshaw**: the first jump-point system (2271). Angeli was terraformed.
- **Davien**: Banu first contact (2438).
- **Elysium**: Elysium IV (Kaleeth) is the lost Tevarin homeworld.
- **Vega**: Vanduul attack in 2945 (Squadron 42).
- **Tiber**: the endless Vanduul front.
- **Orion, Caliban, Virgil**: lost to the Vanduul. Virgil borders Nyx.
- **Hades**: Hadesian ruins of a civilisation that destroyed itself.
- **Garron**: Garron II massacre (Messer-era genocide scandal).
- **Kellog, Ellis, Magnus, Oso, Odin, Nul, Taranis, Tyrol, Goss, Leir**: other frontier systems, good for "rumours from elsewhere".
